Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for the Wordly word search puzzle game.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Wordly, developed and published by SGNexa Labs ("we", "us", or "our"). Wordly is an offline-capable word search puzzle game designed to provide an engaging and relaxing gameplay experience.

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of data Wordly handles, how locally stored gameplay preferences are managed, and how third-party advertising partners process data when you interact with the application.

By installing or using Wordly, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Wordly is designed to function with minimal external data dependencies. To deliver a seamless gaming experience, the app stores essential gameplay preferences locally on your device storage (such as via local key-value storage):

  • Completed levels, high scores, and game progress
  • Audio settings (sound effects and background music)
  • Haptic and vibration feedback preferences
  • In-game custom preferences (theme selections and puzzle state)
Local Isolation: All progress and preference data is stored solely on your device. We do not transmit, back up, or sync this gameplay data to our own dedicated remote servers.

3. Advertising & Analytics

Wordly is a free-to-play application supported by third-party advertising. To deliver and measure ad placements (including banners and interstitial ads), we integrate Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), provided by Google LLC.

When advertisements are requested or displayed, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically gather device-level diagnostic and operational information, including:

  • IP address (used to infer coarse location for regional compliance and relevance)
  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / GAID)
  • App performance metrics, crash logs, and diagnostic telemetry
  • Ad viewability and interaction data (impressions, clicks, frequency capping)
  • Signals used strictly for fraud prevention, security, and spam detection

All diagnostic and advertising data transmitted via the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

For detailed details regarding Google's data processing practices, please consult the Google Privacy Policy and Google’s Play Console Data Safety guidance.

4. Consent & User Choices (EEA / UK / California)

Where applicable by local law (such as the European General Data Protection Regulation and ePrivacy Directive):

  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK may be presented with a Google-certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form to select their preferences regarding personalized or non-personalized advertisements and cookie/mobile identifier use.

5. How We Use Information

Any information processed through the application or third-party SDKs is used strictly for:

  • Operating offline word puzzles and tracking level progression
  • Persisting user-configured sound and haptic preferences
  • Delivering banner and interstitial advertisements
  • Preventing click fraud, bot interactions, and malicious activity
  • Evaluating app stability, error diagnostics, and runtime crashes

6. Information Sharing & Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or gameplay progress.

We do not maintain user accounts or collect personally identifiable information such as names, telephone numbers, home addresses, or email addresses directly inside the application. Data exchange is limited strictly to SDK-level communication with Google Mobile Ads to serve advertisements as permitted by law.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Local App Data

Your puzzle progress and custom settings remain on your device indefinitely until you clear the app's cache/storage from Android Settings or uninstall Wordly. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local storage.

Third-Party Ad Data

Data collected by Google Mobile Ads is retained and expunged in accordance with Google's published data retention schedules and privacy standards.

8. Children's Privacy

Wordly is designed as a general-audience word puzzle game and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under the age defined by local jurisdiction, such as 16 in certain EU countries).

We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a minor has provided data through third-party services within our app, please contact us immediately so we can coordinate appropriate removal.

9. Device Permissions

Wordly requests only the standard Android operating system permissions required for core execution and ad rendering:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google Mobile Ads to retrieve advertisements and verify connectivity.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide optional tactile haptic feedback during gameplay letter selection (can be toggled in settings).

Wordly does not request access to runtime sensitive permissions such as contacts, GPS location, microphone, camera, or storage files.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, legal obligations, or modifications to advertising SDK standards. The "Last updated" date at the top of this document indicates the latest active version.
