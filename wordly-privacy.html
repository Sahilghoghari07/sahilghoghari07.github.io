Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for the Wordly word search puzzle game.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Wordly. Wordly is a word search puzzle game designed to provide an entertaining and engaging puzzle experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how Wordly handles information when you use the application.

By using Wordly, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Wordly may store certain game-related information locally on your device to provide and maintain the game's functionality.

This may include:

  • Game progress and completed levels
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Sound or music preferences
  • Haptic or vibration preferences
  • Other locally stored gameplay preferences
This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to Wordly servers as part of the game's normal functionality.

3. Advertising

Wordly may be supported by advertising.

The application may use Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements, including banner and interstitial advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads is provided by Google and may collect certain information from your device and your interactions with the application in order to provide, measure, maintain, and protect advertising services.

4. Information Collected Through Google Mobile Ads

Depending on the applicable configuration and Google's services, Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • App or device-related identifiers
  • Information about interactions with the application
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used for fraud prevention and security

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

For more information about Google's handling of information, please review:

Google Privacy Policy

Information about Google Mobile Ads and Google Play data disclosure can also be found in Google's developer documentation.

5. Non-Personalized Advertising

Where configured by the application, Wordly may request non-personalized advertisements from Google Mobile Ads.

Non-personalized advertisements are not selected based on a user's previous activity for personalized advertising.

However, non-personalized advertising does not mean that no information is processed. Certain information may still be processed for purposes such as ad delivery, frequency capping, aggregated reporting, security, and fraud prevention.

6. How We Use Information

Information handled by Wordly or its service providers may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing word search and puzzle gameplay functionality
  • Saving local game progress and preferences
  • Displaying advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Maintaining application and advertising functionality
  • Detecting and preventing fraud or abuse
  • Diagnosing technical and performance issues
  • Improving application reliability and user experience

7. Information Sharing

Wordly does not sell your locally stored game progress, game preferences, or other locally stored gameplay information.

Information may be processed or shared by third-party service providers when necessary to provide their services to the application.

In particular, Google Mobile Ads may collect and process information as described in Google's documentation for advertising, reporting, security, and fraud prevention purposes.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the application.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local application data

Game progress and other locally stored preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear the application's storage, uninstall Wordly, or otherwise remove the stored data.

If Wordly provides an in-game option to reset or clear applicable game data, you may use that option to remove the corresponding locally stored information.

Advertising-related information

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is handled according to Google's applicable policies, retention practices, and user controls.

Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for additional information.

9. Data Security

Wordly is designed to minimize the information that it directly stores and processes.

Information stored by Wordly locally remains on your device unless another service or operating-system feature processes it.

Data transmitted by third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads is handled using security measures described by those providers, including encryption in transit where applicable.

10. Children's Privacy

Wordly is a general-audience word puzzle game and is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable laws.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through Wordly.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

11. Third-Party Services

Wordly may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, including advertising and related services.

The primary third-party service currently associated with advertising is:

  • Google Mobile Ads

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the applicable policies of these providers.

Google Privacy Policy

12. Permissions

Wordly may request or use device permissions only when they are required for a particular feature or functionality.

The application does not require access to your contacts, messages, or personal files for normal word puzzle gameplay.

Permission availability and behavior may vary depending on your device, operating system, and version of the application.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Wordly, third-party services, applicable laws, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
