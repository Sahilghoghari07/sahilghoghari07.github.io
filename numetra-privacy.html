Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for the Numetra calculator and utility application.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Numetra, developed and published by SGNexa Labs ("we", "us", or "our"). Numetra is an all-in-one calculator and unit conversion tool engineered to deliver accurate, swift, and everyday operational calculations.

We take user privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines what information Numetra stores locally, how third-party advertising partners handle telemetry data, and your rights concerning data retention and deletion.

By installing or using Numetra, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described herein.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Numetra provides features that store operational settings and records locally on your device storage (such as via local asynchronous storage):

  • Calculation records and history logs
  • favorite calculator and conversion categories
  • User interface theme preferences (Light, Dark, or System Default)
  • Haptic feedback and sound preferences
Local Isolation: All calculation records and preferences reside strictly on your device. We do not transmit, back up, or sync your calculations to our own dedicated remote servers.

3. Advertising & Analytics

Numetra is supported by third-party advertising to keep tools accessible free of charge. We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), provided by Google LLC, to serve banner and interstitial advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect certain diagnostic and operational information from your device and your interactions with the app in order to serve, measure, and protect ad operations.

4. Information Processed by Google Mobile Ads

Depending on your operating system version and configuration, Google Mobile Ads may process data such as:

  • IP address (used to infer coarse location for regional compliance and ad delivery)
  • Device and advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Application and interaction telemetry (ad clicks, impressions, viewability)
  • Diagnostic logs, crash metrics, and runtime performance indicators
  • Security and anti-fraud signals

Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted using standard transport encryption (HTTPS/TLS).

For comprehensive details on how Google processes ad data, please review the Google Privacy Policy .

5. Non-Personalized Advertising & Consent (EEA / UK)

Numetra requests non-personalized advertisements where configured. Non-personalized ads are contextual rather than based on previous browsing history.

Even for non-personalized advertising, basic device data is processed for frequency capping, aggregated reporting, and fraud detection. Where mandated by regional regulations (such as GDPR in the EEA and UK), users may be presented with a Google-certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent screen to manage consent preferences.

6. How We Use Information

Information handled by Numetra or integrated SDKs is used strictly to:

  • Deliver calculator and unit converter functionality
  • Store local user calculations and interface preferences
  • Display banner and interstitial advertisements
  • Measure ad delivery performance and prevent click fraud
  • Diagnose runtime issues, bugs, and app performance degradation

7. Information Sharing & Sale

Numetra does not sell, lease, or monetize your calculation history or personal preferences.

We do not require user account registration, passwords, or personal credentials (such as your name, email, or telephone number). Data handling with third parties is restricted solely to Google Mobile Ads for ad delivery and security compliance.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Application Records

Your calculation history remains saved on your device until you decide to remove it. You can clear your calculation history at any time using the in-app "Clear History" button, by clearing the app data in Android Settings, or by uninstalling Numetra.

Advertising Records

Information handled by Google Mobile Ads is retained and managed according to Google’s standard data retention policies and user control settings.

9. Data Security

Numetra is built on a minimal-data architecture. Because calculation data is held locally on your device sandbox, it remains protected by your device's security model. Network traffic initiated by third-party ad providers is encrypted in transit.

10. Children's Privacy

Numetra is a general utility application not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information directly from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through our services, please contact us immediately to have the information investigated and addressed.

11. Third-Party Services

The third-party service integrated into Numetra is:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) - For banner and interstitial ad delivery.

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy .

12. Device Permissions

Numetra requests only standard Android permissions necessary for functional operations:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by Google Mobile Ads to fetch ad inventory and verify active connectivity.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide optional haptic tactile feedback when keypads are pressed.

Numetra does not request access to sensitive hardware or personal user data such as location, contacts, camera, or device storage files.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app features, policy revisions, or regulatory changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will indicate when the policy was revised.
